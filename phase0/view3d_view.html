Calculation and assumptions

Viewpoint: centre of the front façade, at pad level + eye height. Villas are solid vertical extrusions of their actual footprints. No fixed pad-drop threshold is used.

The horizontal window is ±15°. The central window is ±5° horizontally, from −2.5° to +2.5° vertically, centred on the 0° horizon. If the overall vertical window is narrower on either side, the central box shrinks equally on both sides to remain symmetric. The central requirement measures clear area, not a continuous opening.

Obstruction is measured as a fraction of horizontal-angle × vertical-angle area, with overlapping silhouettes counted once. Conservative 0.25° horizontal columns enclose each silhouette; narrow obstructions may be slightly overcounted. Pad fitting and removal use local search, not a maximum-count proof.

Defaults are study assumptions, not surveyed floor or roof heights. Eye height is measured from the pad; include any upper-floor offset here. Terrain, trees, windows and roof shape are not occluders in this building-only model. Terrain still supplies pad references and downhill orientation. Strict side clearance and existing geometry remain unchanged.
