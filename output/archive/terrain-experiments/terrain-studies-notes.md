# Three Rhino terrain studies

Saved model: three-resort-terrain-options.3dm. Original source snapshot: site-before-terrain-studies.3dm.

The original site and module bank remain in their original positions. Three copies are below them, offset by (0,-430), (390,-430), and (780,-430) drawing units.

- 01_GROUPED_A follows the A mountain-cluster image: five selectable groups of four villas.
- 02_FREE_G follows G woodland scattering: twenty individual villas.
- 03_FREE_I follows I garden wandering: twenty individual villas.

Each option contains two guestroom bars, twenty villas, one banquet and one restaurant. Each guestroom bar is an editable assembly of eleven existing guestroom bays. Original bay dimensions are 5 x 14, villa envelope 11 x 23, banquet geometry 19 x 9 (its source annotation slightly exceeds this), and restaurant 20 x 20 drawing units. Geometry has been translated and rotated without scaling; display-only duplicate definitions retain all original module linework and subdivisions.

The selected outer boundary is closed with area 40335.4 drawing units squared. The smaller inner line and source road curves have also been copied. The original document unit label is millimeters despite its meter-like site dimensions; it has not been changed. These files are concept-scale studies, not a survey deliverable.

Terrain is synthetic, identical in baseline form across options, rising generally north/east, with a shallow central fold and blended level building pads. Each terrain mesh has 5081 vertices and 9699 faces and is valid. Contours are at 2-drawing-unit increments. Terrain, contours, boundaries, modules, fills, and labels have separate layers under TERRAIN_STUDIES_20260910. No surveyed elevations are represented.

All 24 building envelopes in each option were checked against the closed boundary and each other, with 1 drawing unit added around each envelope during clearance checks. Group A moved two whole groups by 6 and 12 units to fit the available modules. G moved one villa by 6 units. I moved five villas by 2–6 units. Generated images are used as layout references, not dimensional authorities.

Screenshots: three-terrain-top.jpg and terrain-perspective.jpg. A custom Resort Terrain Study display mode hides triangulation wires for readable shading. Internal circulation has not been modeled in this terrain-and-module pass.
