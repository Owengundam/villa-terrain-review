## Staggered 2

Architectural plan image generation trial, STAGGERED variant 1. Sole input is an empty site boundary, actual labelled contours and a villa scale diagram to its right. Preserve the site contour geometry and boundary exactly; retain the scale diagram at the side. Fill the site with compact staggered villa positions, alternate rows shifted half a unit pitch like cinema seats. Each unit is precisely the same 11 x 23 m rectangle as the side reference, at the same relative site scale, with NO reduction in width or length. Flat 2D orthographic footprints, no perspective roofs or extrusions, simple pale green fill and dark outline. ONLY villas. Critical priority: villa 23 m LONG AXIS is the LOCAL CONTOUR NORMAL, crossing that contour at 90 degrees. Its 11 m short edges run tangent to the nearest contour. Inspect the nearby contour tangent separately for EVERY villa; do not use one repeated site-wide tilt. Where the contour curves, the villa orientation rotates with it. At the right-hand site where contours run nearly vertically, villas must be nearly HORIZONTAL long rectangles, not vertical. Where contours run horizontally, villas are VERTICAL long rectangles. Where contours run diagonally, rotate villas to the perpendicular diagonal. Around the central curved indentation, fan the villas locally so each long axis points across its contour, not along it. Show a thin centerline along each villa's 23 m long axis to make its perpendicular relation visually explicit. The MAIN axis points downhill toward lower labelled contour levels. Staggering changes center positions ONLY and never overrides local perpendicularity. Keep >=3.05 m side-to-side clearances in both villas' local side axes, compact spacing, no overlaps and no footprints outside the site. Ignore all view-obstruction rules and elevation-drop/pad-height rules: this is an initial image proposal to prune later. Use clean regular contour-following offset bands with half-pitch alternate rows. Site centered in drawing area, unit on right, ivory paper, thin green contour lines and red site outline, no trees, no decorative landscape. Title "STAGGERED 2 — LOCAL CONTOUR NORMALS".
