# AI-only arrangement trial — September 15

Built-in image generation. Sole reference: site-unit-input.png, composed from the September 14 live contour export with an 11 × 23 m unit drawn at the same scale outside the site. No prior plan or rendering template supplied.

Outputs: parallel.png, staggered.png, free.png. These are unverified raster proposals: unit proportions and contour-normal orientations drift, especially in the staggered output. Side clearance is prompted, not geometrically certified. No view/elevation pruning performed. The first staggered draft was rejected for contour-parallel orientations; the delivered revision improves orientation but still has size/proportion drift. Retain these as raw image-generation experiments for later geometry reconstruction, resizing and removal.

## parallel

Generate a new villa site arrangement directly on the supplied reference. Use case: architectural plan diagram. The ONLY input is this bare site with labelled contours and a standard unit on the side; there is no prior building arrangement to imitate. Preserve the exact site boundary and contour shapes. Keep the site large and centered in its drawing area and the isolated scale-unit diagram beside it. All in-site villas must use the same 11 m width x 23 m length and identical scale as the side reference: do not shrink units to squeeze more in. Configuration: Organize villa centers in clear parallel rows, allowing individual villas to rotate to follow their local contour normals. Compact orderly aligned rows. Populate the usable site densely with many repeated standard villas while avoiding overlaps and keeping all footprints inside the boundary. Every villa LONG AXIS must be PERPENDICULAR to its nearest local contour, pointing downhill toward decreasing contour labels. This orientation takes priority over perfect row geometry. Maintain at least 3.05 m clear lateral edge-to-edge separation between adjacent villas, measured along each villa's local side axis; aim close to this gap where possible without breaking orientation. Do NOT impose view cones, sightline obstruction rules, minimum elevation drops, pad-height constraints or height-based spacing. Buildings will be removed by an algorithm later; do not preemptively delete them for views/elevation. Only villas, no hotel, restaurant or banquet. Output a clean orthographic top-down planning drawing with fine dark footprint outlines, light sage and white roofs, readable unobscured thin green contour lines and red boundary on ivory paper. Minimal landscape, no trees covering footprints, no perspective, no 3D extrusion. Title "PARALLEL VILLA ARRANGEMENT". Keep the side unit labelled 11 m x 23 m SAME SCALE. Clearly distinguish every villa for later footprint extraction.
